ECOTECHECOFILTER

EcoFilter · water

On-site water treatment — with before-and-after lab analysis

The modular unit integrates into an existing line. The oxidant is ozone generated on site; the filter element cleans itself during operation. The base configuration requires no continuous dosing of chlorine-based reagents or coagulants.

  • Ozone oxidation

    Ozone is generated on site — no delivered oxidants.

  • Self-cleaning microfiltration

    The element is cleaned during operation; backwash water is metered.

  • Digital log

    Pressure, flow and backwash are recorded; sensors are selected for each site.

  • Integration without shutdown

    Bypass, drain, sampling points and emergency stop are built into the design.

Applications

  • 01

    Wells and settlements

    Iron, manganese, turbidity and colour in groundwater.

  • 02

    Ahead of reverse osmosis

    Reduces the load on membranes — complementing reverse osmosis, not replacing it.

  • 03

    Industry and boiler houses

    Process water tailored to each plant's flow scheme.

  • 04

    Municipal systems

    Point-of-use polishing with residual chlorine monitored before and after.

Manufactured to Technical Specification TU 28.29.12-001-70473237-2025 · EAEU Declaration of Conformity N RU Д-RU.РА06.В.32770/25, valid until 23 July 2030

ECOTECHECOMODULE

EcoModule · heat from waste

Turn on-site waste into local heat

EcoModule RM-1 is a high-temperature vortex reactor in a container. It is designed to produce heat for space heating and industrial processes from qualified carbon-containing waste.

  • Prototype built

    Pilot-scale containerised RM-1 prototype; runs at our own research site.

  • Safety confirmed

    Test report No. ФТ0921ПИ-3/117 of 30.09.2021: the design complies with Customs Union Technical Regulation TR CU 010/2011 “On Safety of Machinery and Equipment”.

  • Technical specification

    TU 28.99.39-001-0107994046-2021; catalogue sheet of FSBI “RST” No. 200/135957 of 23.09.2021.

How we work

Measure first. Scale second

We don't sell promises. We offer an on-site trial with acceptance criteria agreed in advance.

  1. 01

    Understand the task

    Water analysis or waste composition, target results, who uses the water or heat.

  2. 02

    Survey the site

    Connection points, measuring points, power supply, residue disposal.

  3. 03

    Agree the programme

    Parameters, methods, laboratory and duration — before any work starts.

  4. 04

    Install and measure

    8–12 weeks of continuous operation with a series of samples taken by the laboratory.

  5. 05

    Decide on facts

    We move to scale-up only once performance is confirmed.

A result counts as proven only after measurement and an issued laboratory report.
